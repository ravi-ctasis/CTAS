# Amazon FBA Grade & Resell Updates 2025

Amazon’s FBA Grade and Resell program helps sellers recover value from returned inventory. In 2025, the program introduced **three key updates** that make it more flexible, efficient, and profitable for sellers:

1. **New Expanded Product Categories**  
2. **Automatic Out-of-Stock SKU Removal**  
3. **Grade and Resell ASIN Inclusion**

---

## 1. New Expanded Product Categories

The program now supports a wider range of products, including:  

- Apparel  
- Shoes  
- Luggage  
- Watches  
- Jewelry  

This expansion allows sellers to **maximize revenue from returns** across a broader selection of items that were previously ineligible. Sellers can capitalize on returned inventory that still holds value, improving overall profitability.

---

## 2. Automatic Out-of-Stock SKU Removal

Managing returns and resold inventory can be time-consuming. With this update, **out-of-stock items are automatically removed** from the Grade and Resell inventory view.  

Benefits include:

- Easier inventory management  
- Less clutter in active listings  
- Focus on items available for resale  

This ensures sellers can quickly identify active opportunities and streamline their inventory workflow.

---

## 3. Grade and Resell ASIN Inclusion

Sellers now have **greater control over which products participate** in the program. By selecting specific ASINs to include:

- Focus on **high-value or durable items**  
- Reduce unnecessary processing of low-potential returns  
- Optimize the resale process and maximize recovery  

This selective approach allows sellers to tailor the program to their inventory strategy instead of enrolling all products automatically.

---

## Benefits at a Glance

| Update | Key Advantage |
|--------|---------------|
| Expanded Product Categories | Recover more value across multiple item types |
| Automatic Out-of-Stock SKU Removal | Simplified inventory management and focus on active listings |
| ASIN Inclusion | Greater control, higher resale efficiency |

---

## Final Thoughts

The 2025 updates make Amazon’s FBA Grade and Resell program **more efficient, strategic, and profitable**. By leveraging expanded categories, automated inventory cleanup, and selective ASIN inclusion, sellers can **turn more returns into revenue**, reduce manual work, and maintain better inventory control.  

Amazon sellers who adopt these updates are well-positioned to **maximize value from returns** and improve overall FBA performance.
